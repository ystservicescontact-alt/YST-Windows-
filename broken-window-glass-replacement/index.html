What to do right now

  1. Keep children and pets away and wear shoes. Small pieces travel further than you think.
  2. Don't pull loose pieces out of the frame with bare hands. Cover the opening from inside with plastic and tape if you can.
  3. Take photos of the window and the damage. You'll need them for insurance.
  4. Call us. We're available 24/7 to secure the opening.

Make it safe, then replace the glass

If the glass can't be replaced on the spot, we secure the opening so your home is closed and safe. Then we measure, order the right glass and come back to install it in your existing frame.

  • Single pane glass in older windows, storm windows and garages
  • Double and triple pane sealed units in most modern windows
  • Tempered safety glass where it's required, like doors and glass close to the floor
  • Laminated glass for extra security

Common causes

  • Balls, stones from a lawn mower, and branches in a storm
  • Break-ins. See emergency glass repair.
  • Thermal cracks that start at the edge of the glass, often in very cold weather
  • A window that slams shut or a frame that has moved

Insurance

Many home insurance policies cover broken glass, especially after a break-in or a storm. We give you photos and a detailed invoice for your claim. See insurance claims.

Common questions

Can you replace only the broken pane?

Yes. We replace the glass or the sealed unit and keep your existing frame.

Is a cracked window dangerous?

It can be. A crack can spread suddenly, especially with cold weather or when the window is opened. Keep it closed and get it looked at.

Do you come at night?

Yes, we're available 24/7. Calls after 10 PM have a $100 after-hours call-out fee.

Will my insurance pay for it?

Many policies cover broken glass. We provide photos and an itemized invoice to help with your claim.
