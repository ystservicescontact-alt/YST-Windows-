When the glass needs replacing

  • Fog or moisture between the panes that you can't wipe off from either side: the seal has failed.
  • Cracked or shattered glass from an impact, a slammed door or a thermal crack.
  • Scratched or etched glass you no longer want to look through.
  • An upgrade: new glass with a Low-E coating and argon fill to keep the room warmer in winter and cooler in summer.

Patio door glass must be safety glass

Glass in doors has to be safety glass, and patio doors use tempered glass. Tempered glass is about four times stronger than regular glass and breaks into small, blunt pieces instead of sharp shards. Tempered glass can't be cut after it's made, so every unit is made to your door's exact size. Read more in tempered vs laminated glass.

How the replacement works

  1. Send us a photo of the door and the problem. We tell you if it's the glass, the rollers or both.
  2. We measure the glass unit and order a new tempered sealed unit to size.
  3. We take the sliding panel out, remove the old glass, and set the new unit with new glazing tape or seals.
  4. We put the panel back, adjust the rollers and check that the door slides and locks properly.

If the door is hard to slide, we usually fix the rollers at the same visit. See patio and sliding door repair.

Glass only, or a new door?

If the frame is straight, the panels slide and the lock works, replacing only the glass costs a fraction of a new door and keeps your trim and flooring untouched. A new door makes sense when the frame is bent, rotted or the door no longer seals. We'll tell you honestly which one you need.

Common questions

Can you replace just the glass in a sliding patio door?

Yes, in most cases. If the frame and panels are in good shape, we replace only the sealed glass unit.

Do I have to replace both panels?

No. We replace only the panel with the failed or broken glass.

Is patio door glass tempered?

It has to be. Glass in doors must be safety glass, and we install tempered sealed units made to your door's size.

Can you fix a cracked patio door today?

We can make it safe the same day. The new tempered unit is made to size, so it's installed once it arrives.
