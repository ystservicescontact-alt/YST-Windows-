Entry and commercial door repair

Doors that stick, drag, drop or won't lock are usually a hinge, lock or adjustment problem. We repair front doors, French doors and commercial doors without replacing them.

Residential doors

  • Front and entry doors that stick, drag or won't latch
  • French doors that have dropped or don't meet in the middle
  • Multi-point locks and handle sets
  • Hinge replacement and door alignment
  • Weatherstripping and door sweeps for drafts

Commercial doors

  • Door closers that slam or won't close
  • Push bars and panic hardware
  • Pivots and hinges on storefront doors
  • Door glass after break-ins, with board-up

For storefront glass, see commercial and storefront glass. For sliding patio doors, see patio door repair.

Common questions

My French doors don't close anymore. Can they be fixed?

Usually yes. One door has typically dropped on its hinges. We re-hang and adjust the doors so they meet and lock again.

Do you repair commercial door closers?

Yes. We adjust and replace door closers, pivots and push bars.
