Repair first, replace when it makes sense

Many front door problems are fixed without a new door: hinges, the latch and strike, weatherstripping, the sweep or the glass. See door repair and front door glass. A new door makes sense when the door or frame is warped, rotted or split, it no longer seals even after new weatherstripping, or you want a different look or better security.

Signs it's time for a new door

  • Drafts or light around the door even after new weatherstripping
  • A door that sticks every season because it has warped
  • Rot or soft spots at the bottom of the door or frame
  • Rust through a steel door skin, or a split wood door
  • A forced door with a damaged frame after a break-in

Doors we install

  • Steel doors: strong and well insulated, a common choice in the GTA.
  • Fibreglass doors: they don't rust or rot and can look like wood.
  • Doors with glass: full, half or small glass inserts, clear or decorative. Glass in doors is tempered safety glass.
  • Sidelites and transoms beside and above the door, for more light.
  • Double entry doors for wider openings.

How installation works

  1. We measure the opening and the frame, and help you choose the door, glass and hardware.
  2. The door is ordered to size.
  3. On install day we remove the old door and frame, set the new one level and square, insulate and seal around it, and install the hardware.
  4. We adjust it so it closes, latches and locks smoothly, then clean up.

Security

A new door is a good time to upgrade the lock. A deadbolt with a reinforced strike plate and long screws into the framing makes a door much harder to force. Multi-point locks that lock the door at several points are another option.

Warranty

Our installation has a 1-year workmanship warranty. The door itself carries the manufacturer's warranty.

Common questions

Do you replace the frame too?

Usually yes. A new door comes pre-hung in its own frame, which seals better than fitting a new slab into an old frame.

Can you match a new door to my sidelites?

We can replace the sidelites along with the door, or match glass to the ones you have.

Should I repair or replace my front door?

If the door and frame are straight and solid, a repair is usually enough. We'll tell you honestly which one you need.
