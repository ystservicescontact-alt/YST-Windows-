Wood windows

Wood window repair

Wood windows can last a lifetime with the right care. We repair rotted sills and sashes, replace glazing putty and glass, and get older wood windows working again.

What we repair

  • Soft or rotted wood in sills, sashes and frames
  • Cracked or missing glazing putty
  • Broken glass in wood sashes and storm windows
  • Sticking sashes and worn hardware

Repair or replace?

Small areas of rot can be cut out and repaired. When a sill or sash is badly rotted, we can replace that piece rather than the whole window. If the frame is beyond saving, we'll tell you and quote a replacement.

Keep water out

Rot starts where water sits. After repairing, we seal and paint-ready the wood and check the caulking around the window so it doesn't come back.
