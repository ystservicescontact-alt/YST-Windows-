Storm & screen doors

Storm door repair

A storm door protects your front door from Toronto winters and lets in a breeze in summer. When it slams, sags or won't latch, we fix it. When it's past fixing, we install a new one.

Storm door problems we fix

  • Slams shut or won't close: a worn or badly adjusted door closer (the pneumatic cylinder at the top or bottom)
  • Blows open in the wind: a missing or broken wind chain, or a weak closer
  • Won't latch or lock: a worn handle set, latch or strike
  • Sags or drags: loose hinges or a bent hinge rail
  • Drafts at the bottom: a worn door sweep or expander
  • Broken glass or torn screen in the door panel

Brands and styles we repair

We repair full-view, self-storing, ventilating and retractable-screen storm doors from most brands, including Andersen, Larson and Forever, and doors made by local Ontario manufacturers. Most repairs are done in one visit, and we give you a clear price before any work starts.

Glass and screen panels

Many storm doors have interchangeable panels: glass for winter, screen for summer. We replace cracked glass panels and torn screens, and fix panels that won't stay in place. Some doors have a retractable screen built in; we repair those too.

Repair or replace?

If the frame is bent, the corners have separated or the door is badly corroded, a new door is usually better value. We'll tell you honestly. See new storm doors, made in Toronto and installed made to measure.

Retractable screens

A retractable screen rolls away when you don't need it, so it doesn't block your view or your entry door. A good choice for front doors, patio doors and French doors. See also window and door screens.

Storm door slamming every time? Before replacing the closer, check the adjustment screw at the end of the cylinder. Turning it slows or speeds up how fast the door closes.

Common questions

Can you fix a storm door that slams shut?

Usually yes. It's normally the door closer: we adjust it or replace it so the door closes gently and latches.

Do you install new storm doors?

Yes. We measure the opening and install new storm and screen doors, including retractable screen models.

Can you replace the glass in my storm door?

Yes. We replace glass panels and screen panels in most storm doors.
