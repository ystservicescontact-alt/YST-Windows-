Window caulking and draft repair

Old caulking cracks and pulls away from the brick or siding. We remove it and reseal the window so wind and water stay out.

Call (365) 340-4760 Send photos for a quote

Signs your windows need resealing

  • Caulking outside is cracked, gapped, crumbling or missing
  • You feel cold air around the frame on windy days
  • Water stains on the sill, drywall or ceiling below a window
  • Weatherstripping on the sash is flattened, torn or missing

How we do it

We cut out the old caulking completely, clean the joint, add backer rod where the gap is deep, and apply a high quality exterior sealant suited to Canadian winters. Caulking over old caulking doesn't last, so we don't do it.

Inside the window, we replace worn weatherstripping so the sash seals when it's closed and locked.

Best time to do it

Exterior sealants need dry weather above freezing to cure properly, so spring through late fall is ideal. If you have an active leak in winter, call us and we'll find a solution.

Questions

Common questions

How often should windows be recaulked?

It depends on the product and sun exposure, but exterior caulking often needs replacing every 10 years or so. Check it every spring.

Can you fix a window that leaks when it rains?

Yes. We find where the water is getting in, whether it's the caulking, the frame joints or the glass seal, and repair it.
