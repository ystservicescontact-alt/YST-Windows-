What is FAPIM?

FAPIM is an Italian manufacturer of hardware for aluminum windows and doors, based in Altopascio, Tuscany. The name stands for Fabbrica Accessori Per Infissi Metallici, "factory of accessories for metal windows and doors". It was founded in 1974, has a catalogue of around 18,000 parts and exports most of its production to more than 100 countries.

Why FAPIM windows are in GTA condos

Many condo towers in Downtown Toronto and Mississauga are built with aluminum window walls and curtain walls. FAPIM makes a hardware line for exactly these systems, called Vista, with arms sized in inches and tested to the North American AAMA standard. You'll typically find it on the opening vents of condo windows: the top-hung or awning panels that push out at the bottom.

The parts in a FAPIM condo window

  • Stay arms (friction hinges): stainless steel arms on each side of the vent that let it open and hold its position. They come in lengths from about 14 to 28 inches, and larger windows use six-bar arms instead of four-bar.
  • Handle and cremone: a die-cast aluminum handle with a gear inside that drives locking rods along the frame.
  • Keepers and corner locks: the parts on the frame that the locking points hook into, with shims for adjustment.
  • Opening limiters: safety stops that keep the vent from opening too far on high floors.

Common FAPIM problems

  • The vent won't stay open or drops shut: the friction sliders in the stay arms are worn.
  • A bent or twisted arm: wind caught the open vent. The window may not close square or may rub.
  • The handle turns but doesn't lock: worn gear, spring or rack in the handle, or keepers out of adjustment.
  • Drafts and whistling: the vent no longer pulls tight against the seals.
  • Loose or missing screws on the arm brackets, which is dangerous on a high floor.

Why genuine FAPIM parts matter

FAPIM hardware is made to exact sizes for specific aluminum profiles. Generic arms and handles from North American suppliers often don't match the length, the screw positions or the profile, so the window ends up patched, misaligned or unsafe. FAPIM has no local parts distribution in Canada, so we import genuine FAPIM parts from Italy and install them so the window works as it was designed.

How to tell if your window is FAPIM

  1. Open the vent and look at the metal arm on the side or the bracket where it meets the frame.
  2. Look for the FAPIM name stamped into the metal.
  3. Measure the arm when the window is closed, in inches, if you can do it safely.
  4. Text us a photo of the arm and the handle. We'll identify the parts.
