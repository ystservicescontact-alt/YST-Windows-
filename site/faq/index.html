FAQ

Window and door repair questions

Straight answers to what GTA homeowners, condo owners and businesses ask us most. Don't see yours? Text us a photo.

General

Why is there fog between my window panes?

The edge seal of the sealed glass unit has failed and humid air got in. It can't be wiped off. The fix is a new sealed unit installed in your existing frame.

Is condensation on the inside of the glass the same problem?

No. Condensation on the room side in winter is usually high indoor humidity. If you can wipe it off, the seal is fine.

Do you need to replace the frame?

Usually not. If the frame is in good condition we replace only the glass or the broken part.

Can you give me a price from a photo?

Often, yes. Text us a photo of the whole window and a close-up of the problem. For sealed units we confirm the exact size when we measure.

Do you offer emergency service?

Yes. We're available 24/7 for broken windows and glass after a break-in, storm or accident. We secure the opening first, then replace the glass. Calls after 10 PM have a $100 after-hours call-out fee.

Which areas do you serve?

Toronto and the GTA, including Mississauga, Brampton, Vaughan, Markham, Richmond Hill, North York, Scarborough, Etobicoke, Oakville, Newmarket and Durham Region.

Do you work on all brands?

Yes. We repair windows and patio doors from most manufacturers, in homes, condos and commercial buildings.

Are you insured, and is the work guaranteed?

Yes. We are fully insured and WSIB covered, and our workmanship is backed by a 1-year warranty.

Can you come the same day?

Often, yes. We offer same-day appointments when the schedule allows. Call us and we'll give you the earliest time.

Glass

Can you fix a foggy window without replacing the whole window?

Yes. In most cases we replace only the sealed glass unit and keep your existing frame and sash.

How long does it take?

We measure first. The new sealed unit is made to order, which usually takes a few days, and installation is typically under an hour per window.

Can you replace just the glass in a window?

Yes. Replacing only the glass is our most common job. The frame stays in place.

Is it safe to leave a cracked window until you come?

A small crack can usually wait a few days. Cover it with clear packing tape on both sides. If glass is loose or the opening is exposed, call us for emergency board-up.

Can arched glass be replaced without replacing the window?

Yes. We have a new sealed unit made to the exact shape and install it in the existing frame.

Why does shaped glass take longer?

Each piece is made to the exact shape of your opening, often from a template, so it takes longer to make than a standard size.

Can you replace just the glass in my front door?

Yes. Most steel and fibreglass doors have a removable glass insert that can be replaced on its own.

Will a new insert fit my door?

We measure the opening and the frame around the glass, then match an insert to your door.

Is a foggy skylight leaking?

Not necessarily. Fog between the panes means the glass seal has failed. A new sealed unit fixes it.

Why does my skylight drip only in winter?

Usually condensation: warm indoor air hitting cold glass. Ice dams on the roof can also cause winter leaks.

Can you replace just the glass in a sliding patio door?

Yes, in most cases. If the frame and panels are in good shape, we replace only the sealed glass unit.

Do I have to replace both panels?

No. We replace only the panel with the failed or broken glass.

Can you replace only the broken pane?

Yes. We replace the glass or the sealed unit and keep your existing frame.

Is a cracked window dangerous?

It can be. A crack can spread suddenly, especially with cold weather or when the window is opened. Keep it closed and get it looked at.

Hardware

Where can I get FAPIM window parts in Toronto?

FAPIM parts are hard to find in Canada. We import genuine FAPIM parts directly from Italy and install them.

My FAPIM window won't stay open. Can it be fixed?

Yes. This usually means the friction stay arms are worn or bent. We replace them with genuine FAPIM stays.

How much does window crank replacement cost?

Most crank replacements cost $150–$400, depending on the type of crank operator. Condo windows usually cost more because the work is harder to access and more complex. Send us a photo for an exact price.

My crank turns but the window doesn't move. What's wrong?

The gears inside the operator are stripped. The fix is a new crank operator, not a new window.

My crank handle spins without opening the window. Is it the handle?

Sometimes. If the handle is loose on the shaft, it's the handle. If the shaft turns but the window doesn't move, it's the operator. We check and replace the right part.

Can I buy a handle myself?

You can, but many don't fit because of spline size and shape. A photo of yours lets us bring the right one.

Should both hinges be replaced?

Usually yes. The top and bottom hinges wear together, and replacing both keeps the sash square.

Why does the bottom corner of my casement window drag?

Worn or bent hinges let the sash drop, so the corner rubs the frame. New hinges lift it back into place.

Can you add a lock to a window that doesn't have one?

In many cases, yes. We can add keyed locks or secondary locks to most window types.

My window only locks at one point. Is that a problem?

Yes. It leaves the window loose, letting in drafts and making it easier to force. Usually a keeper or the lock bar needs adjusting or replacing.

Are latches expensive to replace?

No. Latches are usually one of the smallest repairs. Send us a photo and we'll quote it.

Can you replace a tilt latch without replacing the sash?

Yes. Tilt latches are separate parts that we replace in the existing sash.

Why does my tilt and turn window fall open on one side?

The handle was turned to the wrong position while the window was open, or the mechanism is out of adjustment. Support the window and call us. It can usually be put back and adjusted without new parts.

Can you get parts for European windows?

Yes. We find matching or compatible parts for most tilt and turn hardware.

Doors

Why is my sliding door so hard to open?

Usually worn rollers or a dirty, damaged track. Replacing the rollers and cleaning the track normally fixes it.

Can you replace the glass in a patio door?

Yes. We replace the tempered sealed unit in the existing door panel.

My French doors don't close anymore. Can they be fixed?

Usually yes. One door has typically dropped on its hinges. We re-hang and adjust the doors so they meet and lock again.

Do you repair commercial door closers?

Yes. We adjust and replace door closers, pivots and push bars.

How much does a new storm door cost?

A new storm door, supplied and installed, typically costs $1,200–$1,600 depending on the style, size, glass and hardware. We give you an exact price after measuring.

How long does it take to get a new storm door?

We measure first and the door is made to fit your opening. We'll give you the timeline with your quote. Installation itself usually takes a few hours.

Can you fix a storm door that slams shut?

Usually yes. It's normally the door closer: we adjust it or replace it so the door closes gently and latches.

Do you install new storm doors?

Yes. We measure the opening and install new storm and screen doors, including retractable screen models.

How long does a roller replacement take?

Usually a single visit. We bring rollers that match your door, or order the exact part if it's uncommon.

Can you fix a sliding door that came off the track?

Yes. We set it back, replace worn rollers and check the track so it stays on.

Can a new patio door fit my existing opening?

Yes. The door is ordered to the size of your opening. If you want a wider door, the opening has to be changed, and a permit may be needed.

My patio door is hard to slide. Do I need a new one?

Usually not. New rollers and a clean track fix most sliding doors.

Do you replace the frame too?

Usually yes. A new door comes pre-hung in its own frame, which seals better than fitting a new slab into an old frame.

Can you match a new door to my sidelites?

We can replace the sidelites along with the door, or match glass to the ones you have.

My storefront door slams. Can it be adjusted?

Usually yes. Door closers have adjustment valves for closing speed and latching. If the closer leaks oil, it needs replacing.

Do you replace glass in aluminum doors?

Yes. We replace glass in aluminum storefront doors and the storefront panels around them.

Seals, leaks & frames

How often should windows be recaulked?

It depends on the product and sun exposure, but exterior caulking often needs replacing every 10 years or so. Check it every spring.

Can you fix a window that leaks when it rains?

Yes. We find where the water is getting in, whether it's the caulking, the frame joints or the glass seal, and repair it.

Why does my window only leak in heavy rain?

Usually because the drainage can't keep up: weep holes are blocked or the sill slopes the wrong way. We find the cause and fix it.

Why is there water on the inside of my window in winter?

Usually it's condensation from humid indoor air. Lower humidity and better air flow help. If water appears after rain, it's a leak.

Can a rotted window sill be repaired?

Often, yes. Small areas can be repaired. Badly rotted sills can be replaced without replacing the whole window.

Can you replace the glass in a wood window?

Yes. We replace single pane glass and sealed units in wood sashes and reglaze them.

Can you make a screen for a window that never had one?

In most cases yes. We measure the opening and make a screen to fit.

My sliding screen door keeps falling off the track. Can it be fixed?

Usually yes. Worn or misadjusted rollers are the common cause. We replace the rollers and adjust the door so it stays on the track.

Replacement, commercial & emergency

Should I repair or replace my windows?

If the frame is solid, repair is usually cheaper and faster. If the frame is damaged or several parts have failed, replacement makes more sense. We'll recommend honestly.

Do you replace just one window?

Yes. We replace single windows as well as whole houses.

Can you secure a broken storefront the same day?

Call us as soon as it happens. We'll cover the opening as quickly as possible and come back with the new glass.

Do you work with insurance claims?

Yes. We provide a detailed quote and invoice and photos you can send to your insurer.

Is there an extra charge at night?

Emergency calls after 10 PM have a $100 after-hours call-out fee. Before 10 PM there's no extra charge for the visit.

How fast can you come?

It depends on the day and your location. Call us and we'll give you a real arrival time.

Do I need my building's permission to fix my windows?

Check with your property manager first. Windows are often common elements, and many buildings want to approve the contractor or handle the repair themselves.

Can you fix condo windows from inside the unit?

Most hardware repairs, like vent arms, handles, cranks and balcony door rollers, are done from inside your unit.

Can you replace the glass in a small basement window?

Yes. We replace the glass or sealed unit in your existing frame, whatever the size.

My basement window leaks when it rains. Is it the window?

Sometimes it's the window, often it's the caulking or a window well that doesn't drain. We check both.
