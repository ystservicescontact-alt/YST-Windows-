Commercial services

  • Storefront glass replacement, tempered and laminated
  • Foggy and broken sealed units in office and condo windows
  • Commercial door repair: closers, pivots, handles and glass
  • Emergency board-up after break-ins or accidents
  • Hardware and caulking maintenance for property managers

Working around your business

We schedule around opening hours where possible, secure the opening the same day when glass is broken, and provide clear quotes and invoices for owners, tenants and insurers.

Property managers

If you manage multiple units or buildings, we can handle repeat work: survey failed sealed units across a building, give one quote, and replace them in batches.

What affects the price

Every window is different. The price depends on:

  • Glass size and thickness
  • Tempered or laminated glass
  • Emergency board-up needed first
  • After-hours work to avoid closing your business
