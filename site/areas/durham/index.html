Window repair in Durham Region

Durham Region stretches from Pickering and Ajax to Clarington and north to Lake Simcoe. The lakeshore towns have lots of 1980s to 2000s homes whose windows are now failing, while the northern townships have older farmhouses and cottages with their own glass and hardware needs.
