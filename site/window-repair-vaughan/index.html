Window repair across Vaughan

Many Vaughan homes in Woodbridge, Maple and Thornhill were built in the 1980s through the 2000s with large casement windows, feature picture windows and wide patio doors. After 15 to 25 years, those sealed units and crank mechanisms are the first things to go.

Replacing the failed glass in your existing frame keeps the look of your home and costs far less than new windows.
