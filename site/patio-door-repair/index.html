Patio and sliding door repair in the GTA

Common patio door problems we fix

  • Hard to slide or grinding: worn or broken rollers under the door panel
  • Door jumps off the track: damaged track or rollers set too low
  • Won't lock or latch: misaligned or broken lock and keeper
  • Loose or broken handle
  • Fog between the glass or a cracked panel
  • Drafts and water at the bottom: worn weatherstripping and sill seals
  • Torn or sagging screen door

Rollers: the usual culprit

Most sliding doors ride on two small roller assemblies at the bottom of the moving panel. Over the years the wheels flatten, crack or seize, and grit in the track speeds it up. We lift the panel out, replace the rollers, clean and repair the track, and adjust the height so the door glides and locks properly.

Patio door glass

Patio door glass is large and must be tempered. We replace foggy or broken sealed units in the existing panel, so you don't need a whole new door.

When a new door makes sense

If the frame is rotted, badly bent or the door is very old and leaky, we can quote a replacement as well. Most of the time, a repair gets years more out of the door you have.

Questions

Common questions

Why is my sliding door so hard to open?

Usually worn rollers or a dirty, damaged track. Replacing the rollers and cleaning the track normally fixes it.

Can you replace the glass in a patio door?

Yes. We replace the tempered sealed unit in the existing door panel.

Do you work on all patio door brands?

We repair most brands. A photo of the door and any label on the frame helps us bring the right parts.
