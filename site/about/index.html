12-point window check

Every visit includes a 12-point check of your window, so you know about any other problem before it gets worse.

Our 12-point window check

Every visit includes a check of the whole window, not just the part you called about:

  1. Glass seal: fog, haze or mineral streaks between the panes
  2. Glass: cracks, chips and stress cracks
  3. Frame: rot, cracks, warping or loose corners
  4. Sash alignment: does it sit square and close evenly
  5. Hinges: bends, rust and sagging
  6. Crank operator: gears, handle and smooth operation
  7. Locks and keepers: do they pull the sash tight
  8. Weatherstripping: gaps, tears and flattening
  9. Exterior caulking: cracks, gaps and missing sealant
  10. Drainage: weep holes clear and the sill draining away
  11. Screen: tears, fit and frame condition
  12. Final test: opens, closes and locks properly
