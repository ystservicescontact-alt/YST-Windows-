Window repair across Mississauga

A large share of Mississauga's houses were built from the 1970s to the 2000s, in neighbourhoods like Erin Mills, Meadowvale, Churchill Meadows and Streetsville. Many of those homes still have their original sealed units and casement hardware, which is exactly the age when seals start to fail and crank operators wear out.

We replace the failed glass or part and keep the frame, so you avoid the cost and mess of full replacement.

What we fix most often in Mississauga

  • Foggy double pane glass in casement and picture windows
  • Stripped crank operators and bent hinges on casements
  • Patio doors that drag on worn rollers
  • Cracked glass and drafty, poorly sealed frames
  • Sealed units and hardware in condos and townhouses

Neighbourhoods we serve

Port Credit, Lorne Park, Clarkson, Erin Mills, Meadowvale, Churchill Meadows, Streetsville, Cooksville, City Centre, Malton and the rest of Mississauga.
