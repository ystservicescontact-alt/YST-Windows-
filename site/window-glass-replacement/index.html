Broken & cracked glass

Window glass replacement in Toronto and the GTA

A cracked pane doesn't mean a new window. We replace the glass in your existing frame, matched to the original.

Glass we replace

  • Sealed double and triple pane units in vinyl, aluminum, wood and fibreglass frames
  • Single pane glass in older windows, storm windows, cabinets and doors
  • Tempered safety glass, which is required in doors and some locations near floors, stairs and bathtubs
  • Laminated glass for security and noise reduction
  • Obscure and frosted glass for bathrooms and entry doors

Common causes of cracked glass

Stones from lawn mowers, balls, break-ins and storms are the obvious ones. In winter we also see thermal stress cracks: a crack that starts at the edge of the glass, often with no impact at all, caused by a big temperature difference between the centre of the glass and its edge.

What happens on a glass replacement

If the window is open to the weather, we can cover it first. Then we measure the glass to the 1/16 of an inch, order the new pane or sealed unit, and come back to install it. We remove the broken glass safely, clean the frame, set the new glass with new glazing tape or seals, and check that the sash opens, closes and locks.

Repair or replace the window?

If the frame is solid and square, replacing the glass is almost always the better value. If the frame is rotted, warped or the window no longer closes, a full window replacement may make more sense. We'll tell you which one you need.

Questions

Common questions

Can you replace just the glass in a window?

Yes. Replacing only the glass is our most common job. The frame stays in place.

Is it safe to leave a cracked window until you come?

A small crack can usually wait a few days. Cover it with clear packing tape on both sides. If glass is loose or the opening is exposed, call us for emergency board-up.

Do I need tempered glass?

Doors, sidelights and glass close to the floor, stairs or a bathtub usually need safety glass. We check and use the right type.
