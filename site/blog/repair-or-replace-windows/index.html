Should I repair or replace my windows?

A simple guide for GTA homeowners: when a window repair makes sense, when replacement does, and how to tell the difference.

Window companies that only sell new windows will usually recommend replacement. But most window problems come down to one failed part, and fixing that part costs a fraction of a new window.

Repair makes sense when

  • The glass is foggy or cracked, but the frame is solid
  • A crank, hinge, handle, lock or latch is broken
  • Drafts come from worn weatherstripping or old caulking
  • A patio door drags on worn rollers

Replacement makes sense when

  • The frame is rotted, soft or cracked through
  • The window is badly out of square and can't be adjusted
  • Several parts have failed at once and repairs add up close to a new window
  • You want a different style or size of window

What repair saves you

Besides cost, a repair avoids removing interior trim, patching drywall and repainting. It's usually done in under an hour per window. And you can upgrade the glass while you're at it with Low-E and argon.
