Window and glass repair in Port Perry

We repair windows and patio doors in homes, condos and businesses across Durham Region. Port Perry is part of the Township of Scugog. Most problems come down to one failed part, so we replace the glass or hardware and keep your frame, trim and walls as they are.

About windows in Durham Region

Durham Region stretches from Pickering and Ajax to Clarington and north to Lake Simcoe. The lakeshore towns have lots of 1980s to 2000s homes whose windows are now failing, while the northern townships have older farmhouses and cottages with their own glass and hardware needs.

What we fix in Port Perry

  • Foggy Window Repair: Moisture between the panes means the seal failed. We replace the sealed glass unit and keep your frame.
  • Window Glass Replacement: Cracked, chipped or broken glass replaced in your existing frame, single or double pane.
  • Patio & Sliding Door Repair: Sliding doors that stick, drag or won't lock. New rollers, tracks, handles and door glass.
  • Window Crank & Hardware Repair: Casement cranks, hinges, locks and handles that are stiff, stripped or broken. Cranks from $150.
  • Window Caulking & Drafts: Cracked exterior caulking and worn weatherstripping let in cold air and water. We reseal them.
  • Window & Door Screens: Torn mesh, bent frames and missing screens, made to measure.
  • Window Replacement: When the frame is past saving, we supply and install new energy-efficient windows.
  • Commercial & Storefront Glass: Storefront glass, office windows and commercial doors for shops, offices and buildings.
  • Emergency Glass Repair: Broken window after a break-in or storm? We're available 24/7 to secure the opening and replace the glass.
  • FAPIM Window Repair: Italian FAPIM windows in GTA condos repaired with genuine parts imported from Italy.
  • Tilt & Turn Window Repair: European tilt and turn windows that won't lock, tilt or close tight. Handles, gears and hinges repaired.
  • Window Handle Repair: Loose, cracked or stripped window handles and crank handles replaced to match.
  • Window Hinge Repair: Bent, seized or sagging casement and awning hinges replaced so the window closes square.
  • Window Lock Repair: Window locks and multi-point locks that won't latch or pull the window tight.
  • Window Latch Repair: Tilt latches, sash latches and slider latches that are broken or won't catch.
  • Entry & Commercial Door Repair: Front doors, French doors and commercial doors that stick, drop or won't lock.
  • Front Door Glass: Door glass inserts, sidelites and transoms replaced without replacing the door.
  • Curved & Shaped Glass: Arched, half-round, trapezoid and custom-shaped sealed units made to fit.
  • Wood Window Repair: Rotted sills, damaged sashes and worn wood windows repaired and restored.
  • Window Leaks & Drainage: Water coming in around or under a window. We find the source and fix the drainage and seals.
  • Storm & Screen Doors: Storm doors that slam, sag or won't latch, repaired. New storm and screen doors installed.
  • Skylight Repair: Leaking, foggy or cracked skylight glass repaired or replaced without a full tear-out.

Common questions

Do you do window repair in Port Perry?

Yes. Port Perry is part of our Durham Region service area. Call or text us and we'll book a time that works.

Can you give me a price from a photo?

Usually, yes. Text us a photo of the whole window and a close-up of the problem. For sealed glass we confirm the exact size when we measure.

Do I need a new window or just a repair?

If the frame is solid, a new sealed unit or a new crank, hinge or roller is usually all you need, at a fraction of the cost of a new window.

Which window brands do you repair?

Most of them, including the windows builders install in new subdivisions. See our brands page or send us a photo of the label.
