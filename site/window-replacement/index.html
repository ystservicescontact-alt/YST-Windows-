When replacement is the right call

  • The frame or sill is rotted, soft or cracked
  • The window is out of square and won't close or lock
  • Several parts have failed at once and repairs would cost close to a new window
  • You want a different style, size or opening

If only the glass or hardware has failed, a glass or hardware repair is usually the better value. We'll show you both options.

Windows we install

Casement, awning, single and double hung, sliders, picture windows and bay and bow windows, in vinyl and other frame materials. Glass options include double or triple pane, Low-E coatings and argon fill, chosen for the GTA's cold winters and hot summers.

How installation works

We measure every opening, order the windows to size, and install them with proper insulation, flashing and exterior caulking. We finish the interior trim and clean up.

Ontario and federal energy programs change often. Ask us about any rebate currently available for window upgrades before you buy.

What affects the price

Every window is different. The price depends on:

  • Window size, style and opening type
  • Frame material and colour
  • Glass package: double or triple pane, Low-E, argon
  • Interior and exterior finishing

Common questions

Should I repair or replace my windows?

If the frame is solid, repair is usually cheaper and faster. If the frame is damaged or several parts have failed, replacement makes more sense. We'll recommend honestly.

Do you replace just one window?

Yes. We replace single windows as well as whole houses.
