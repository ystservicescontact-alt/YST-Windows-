Window repair across Richmond Hill

Richmond Hill homes, especially in Bayview Hill, Mill Pond and Jefferson, often have large windows, two-storey foyer glass and wide patio doors. Big sealed units are the most likely to fog when their seals fail, and they're expensive to replace as full windows. We replace just the glass.

What we fix most often in Richmond Hill

  • Large foggy sealed units, including high and hard-to-reach windows
  • Casement crank and hinge repair
  • Patio door glass, rollers and locks
  • Exterior caulking and drafty windows

Neighbourhoods we serve

Mill Pond, Bayview Hill, Jefferson, Oak Ridges, Richvale, Elgin Mills, Rouge Woods, Langstaff and Westbrook.
