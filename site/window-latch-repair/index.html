Latches we replace

  • Tilt latches at the top of single and double hung sashes
  • Sash locks where the two sashes meet
  • Slider latches on horizontal sliding windows
  • Balances, the springs that hold a hung window up when it's open

Window won't stay open?

If a hung window slides back down when you open it, the balance is broken, not the latch. We replace balances too.

Signs a latch needs replacing

  • The latch spins or flops and doesn't catch
  • A hung window won't tilt in for cleaning, or won't tilt back
  • A plastic tilt latch is cracked or the spring is gone
  • A sliding window won't stay closed

Matching the part

Latches differ by window brand, size, hand (left or right) and colour. We look at the window brand and the latch shape, then bring the right part so it fits the existing holes and looks like it came with the window.

Fix it before it gets worse

A broken tilt latch can let a sash fall inward when it's opened, and a sliding window that won't latch is a security problem. Latches are small parts, so it's a quick repair. Combine it with other small fixes in one visit.

Common questions

Are latches expensive to replace?

No. Latches are usually one of the smallest repairs. Send us a photo and we'll quote it.

Can you replace a tilt latch without replacing the sash?

Yes. Tilt latches are separate parts that we replace in the existing sash.

My sliding window won't stay closed. Is it the latch?

Often. It can also be a worn roller or track. We check the window and replace what's needed.
