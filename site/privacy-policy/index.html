Privacy

Privacy policy

Last updated October 2026. This policy explains what personal information we collect when you contact us, why we collect it and how we protect it.

What we collect

When you fill in a form, call, text or message us, we may collect:

  • Your name, phone number, email address and city or address
  • Details about your windows or doors and the work you need
  • Photos you send us of your windows, doors or property
  • How you heard about us

If website analytics are turned on, we also collect general information about how visitors use the site, such as pages viewed and the type of device, using cookies. This does not identify you by name.

Why we collect it

  • To answer your request and give you a quote
  • To schedule, carry out and invoice the work
  • To provide warranty service
  • To improve our website and services

We only use your information for these purposes. We don't sell or rent it to anyone.

Who we share it with

We share information only with service providers that help us run the business, such as our website host and form provider, email and phone services, and analytics, and only as needed for them to provide their service. We may also share information if the law requires it, or with your insurer if you ask us to help with a claim.

Photos

Photos you send are used to quote and plan your job. We will not publish photos of your property on our website or social media without your permission, and we never publish faces, house numbers or licence plates.

How long we keep it

We keep your information for as long as we need it to provide the service, honour warranties and meet legal and tax requirements, and then delete it.

Your choices and rights

You can ask to see the personal information we hold about you, ask us to correct it, or withdraw your consent and ask us to delete it, subject to legal requirements. To do this, contact us at (365) 340-4760.

Security

We take reasonable steps to protect your information from loss, misuse and unauthorized access. No method of sending information over the internet is completely secure.

Changes

We may update this policy from time to time. The date at the top shows when it was last changed.

Contact

Questions about privacy? Call or text us at (365) 340-4760.
