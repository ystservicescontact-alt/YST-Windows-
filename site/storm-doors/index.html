New storm doors

Storm doors, supplied and installed

A storm door adds a second barrier against wind, rain and snow, lets in light, and protects your front door. We supply heavy-duty aluminum storm doors made in Toronto and install them made to measure.

Why add a storm door

  • Less heat loss: a second door keeps wind and cold off your entry door in winter
  • Protects your front door from rain, snow and sun, so it lasts longer
  • Light and fresh air: see outside, or swap to a screen for a summer breeze
  • Extra security with an optional key lock and deadbolt

Built for Canadian winters

The storm doors we install are made in Toronto by Varga Windows, a local aluminum manufacturer. Standard features include:

  • 1½-inch or 2-inch thick heavy-duty aluminum frames
  • Tempered safety glass
  • Double weatherstripping against drafts
  • Heavy crossbars and strong corners so the door stays rigid and square
  • Heavy-duty door closer
  • Heavy-duty, non-corrosive, self-lubricating hinges
  • Inserts that stop at different heights for ventilation, removable from inside for cleaning

Styles

Full View

One large glass panel for the most light and the clearest view of your front door. Swap the glass for a screen panel in summer.

Hi-View

Glass on top with a solid kick panel below, which stands up to kids, pets and boots.

One-Lite

A single glass opening in a classic frame.

Tri-Lite

Three glass sections for a traditional look.

Self-Storing

Glass and screen are stored inside the door. Slide the glass down to let in air, with no panels to swap or store.

Decorative glass

Sandblasted and decorative glass options for more privacy and a custom look.

Colours, handles and locks

Available in white, black, sandalwood, ivory, pearl grey, chocolate brown, mill and commercial brown. Choose from different handles, sweeps and glass options, with DX pull handles and an optional colour-matched key lock and deadbolt. Embossed stucco kick panels are available on some styles.

How installation works

  1. We measure your door opening and help you choose the style, colour and hardware.
  2. Your door is made to fit your opening.
  3. We remove the old storm door if you have one, and take it away.
  4. We install and adjust it: the frame square, the closer set so the door closes gently, and the weatherstripping sealed.

Common questions

How long does it take to get a new storm door?

We measure first and the door is made to fit your opening. We'll give you the timeline with your quote. Installation itself usually takes a few hours.

Do you take away my old storm door?

Yes. We remove your old storm door and take it away when we install the new one.

Which storm door style is best?

Full View gives the most light and view. Self-Storing is the easiest for ventilation because the screen is built in. Hi-View is the toughest choice for busy entrances with kids and pets.

What colours are available?

White, black, sandalwood, ivory, pearl grey, chocolate brown, mill and commercial brown.
