# Image prompts for the animated explainers

Use these in your AI image tool, the same one you used for the service photos.
Every image: **16:10 (landscape), at least 2400 x 1500 px, photorealistic, no people, no text, no logos, no watermark.**
Save each one under the file name in brackets and send them to Claude.

The animation (fog, gas, crank turning, captions) is added on top in code. So keep the images **clean and still**:
no fog, no damage, nothing moving.

---

## Foggy window (condo)

**1. Main scene `fog-condo.jpg`**
> Photorealistic interior photo of a modern Toronto condo living room, camera facing a large floor-to-ceiling fixed window straight on, eye level. Dark charcoal aluminum window frame, clean double-pane glass, perfectly clear. Through the window: the Toronto skyline in daylight with the CN Tower clearly visible slightly left of centre, Lake Ontario in the distance, a few clouds, soft late-morning light. Light grey walls, light oak floor at the bottom edge. The window takes up the middle 60% of the image, with plain wall on both sides. Sharp focus, realistic reflections, natural colours, shot on a full-frame camera, 24mm, no people, no furniture in front of the window, no text.

**2. Close-up `fog-edge-macro.jpg`**
> Photorealistic macro photo of the bottom corner of a double-pane window glass unit, seen from the side so you can see both sheets of glass, the aluminum spacer bar between them and the black edge sealant around the outside. Studio lighting, neutral grey background, sharp detail, no fog, no damage, no text.

## Window crank (house)

**3. Main scene `crank-house.jpg`**
> Photorealistic interior photo of a white vinyl casement window in a Canadian suburban home, camera facing the window straight on, eye level. The window is fully closed. A white crank handle is visible on the inside sill at the bottom centre of the window. Through the glass: a quiet Toronto-area residential street in autumn, red brick houses with gabled roofs across the street, a red maple tree, a Canadian flag on a porch, sidewalk and parked car. Beige painted wall around the window, white window trim and sill. Soft daylight, sharp focus, realistic glass reflections, 24mm full-frame, no people, no text.

**4. Same scene, window open `crank-house-open.jpg`**
Upload image 3 to the tool and ask it to edit:
> Same photo, same camera, same light. Only change: the casement window sash is open about 20 degrees outward (hinged on the left), so there is a gap on the right side. Everything else identical.

**5. Crank handle alone `crank-handle.png`**
> Product photo of a white plastic casement window crank handle (folding T-handle), seen straight on from the front, on a plain pure white background, soft studio light, sharp, no shadow on the background.
(If your tool can export a transparent background, use that.)

**6. Gear cutaway `crank-gears-stripped.jpg`**
> Photorealistic cutaway close-up of a casement window crank operator gearbox, the cover removed, showing the steel worm screw driving a brass spur gear. Several teeth on the gear are worn down and stripped. Studio lighting, dark neutral background, sharp macro detail, no text.

**7. New gears `crank-gears-new.jpg`**
> Same as image 6, same angle and lighting, but the gear is brand new with all teeth intact and clean.
