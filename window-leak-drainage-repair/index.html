Where window leaks come from

  • Blocked weep holes: small drain holes at the bottom of the frame clog with dirt and bugs, so water backs up inside
  • Failed exterior caulking around the frame
  • Worn seals between the sash and the frame
  • Gaps at the frame corners or a cracked sill

How we fix it

We test the window to find the source, clear and open the drainage, replace seals and weatherstripping, and reseal the outside of the frame. Fixing the cause matters: a leak left alone damages drywall, insulation and framing.

Quick check: look for small slots on the outside bottom edge of your window frame. If they're full of dirt, clearing them may stop the leak.

Is it a leak or condensation?

Water on the inside of the glass in winter is usually condensation from humid indoor air, not a leak. Water that shows up after rain, runs down the wall or stains the drywall under the window is a leak. See condensation between the panes for the difference.

Blocked weep holes

Most windows have small drain holes, called weep holes, in the bottom of the frame. When dirt, bugs or old caulking block them, rain fills the track and spills inside. Clearing them is often the whole fix.

Basement windows and window wells

A window well that doesn't drain fills with water and pushes it through the window. We check the well, the drain and the caulking. See basement window repair.

When it's not the window

Sometimes water comes from the roof, the siding or the flashing above the window. If it's not the window, we'll tell you so you call the right trade.

Common questions

Why does my window only leak in heavy rain?

Usually because the drainage can't keep up: weep holes are blocked or the sill slopes the wrong way. We find the cause and fix it.

Why is there water on the inside of my window in winter?

Usually it's condensation from humid indoor air. Lower humidity and better air flow help. If water appears after rain, it's a leak.

Can you fix a leak without replacing the window?

Usually yes: clearing weep holes, resealing caulking or replacing seals fixes most window leaks.
