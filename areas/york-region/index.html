Window repair in York Region

York Region grew fast from the 1980s on, so many houses here have large casement and picture windows that are now 20 to 40 years old. Foggy sealed units, stripped crank operators and sticking patio doors are the most common calls, from Thornhill up to the shores of Lake Simcoe.
