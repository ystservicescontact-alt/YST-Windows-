Condo window problems we fix

  • Vents that drop shut or won't close tight: worn friction arms and handles, very common on FAPIM hardware in Toronto and Mississauga towers. See FAPIM window repair.
  • Cranks that spin or won't turn: stripped operators on casement and awning vents. See window crank repair.
  • Balcony sliding doors that drag, jump the track or won't lock: rollers, handles and locks. See patio door repair.
  • Foggy or cracked glass in windows and balcony doors. See foggy window repair and patio door glass replacement.
  • Drafts and whistling from worn seals and vents that no longer pull tight.
  • Screens that are torn or missing. See window and door screens.

Working in your building

Condo work has its own rules, and a smooth job starts before we arrive.

  • Tell your property manager. Many buildings want to know which contractor is coming and when.
  • Book the elevator if your building requires it for contractors or large parts like glass units.
  • Proof of coverage. Many buildings ask contractors for proof of insurance and WSIB coverage. We're fully insured and WSIB covered.
  • Parking and access. Let us know about visitor parking and how to reach your unit through the concierge.

Most hardware repairs are done from inside your unit, without scaffolding or a swing stage.

Who pays for condo window repairs?

In many Ontario condos the windows are common elements, so the condo corporation is responsible for most repairs. But your building's declaration decides, and some parts, such as screens or damage you caused, can fall to the owner. Read our guide: Who pays for condo window repairs in Ontario?

What condo repairs cost

Condo repairs usually cost more than the same repair in a house. The hardware is often specialized, high floors take more care, and there is more time spent on access. For example, a window crank replacement is usually $150 to $400, and condo cranks are at the higher end. Send us a photo of the part and we'll give you a price.

Common questions

Do I need my building's permission to fix my windows?

Check with your property manager first. Windows are often common elements, and many buildings want to approve the contractor or handle the repair themselves.

Can you fix condo windows from inside the unit?

Most hardware repairs, like vent arms, handles, cranks and balcony door rollers, are done from inside your unit.

Do you work on FAPIM windows?

Yes. FAPIM hardware is common in Toronto and Mississauga condos, and we import genuine FAPIM parts from Italy.

Why does condo work cost more than a house?

Specialized hardware, high floors and building access all add time. We give you the price up front, from a photo or a visit.
