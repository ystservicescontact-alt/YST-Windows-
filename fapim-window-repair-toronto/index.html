FAPIM windows in Toronto condos

Many of Toronto's condo towers, especially downtown and in midtown, are built with aluminum window walls. The opening vents on those windows often use FAPIM hardware from Italy: stainless steel stay arms, a cremone handle and locking points around the frame. After years of use, wind and freeze and thaw cycles, the arms wear and the locks slip out of adjustment.

What we fix

  • Stay arms that no longer hold the vent open, or that are bent
  • Handles and cremones that turn but don't lock
  • Keepers and corner locks out of adjustment, causing drafts
  • Opening limiters and loose arm brackets

Genuine parts from Italy

FAPIM parts are hard to find in Canada, and generic hardware rarely fits these aluminum profiles. We import genuine FAPIM parts from Italy, so your window is repaired properly instead of patched. Read more about FAPIM window repair.

Buildings and areas we serve

Downtown Toronto, CityPlace, Liberty Village, King West, the Financial District, St. Lawrence, Yonge and Bloor, Yorkville, Midtown and Yonge and Eglinton, North York Centre and condos across the city.
