Questions

Common questions

Why is there fog or moisture between my window panes?

Double and triple pane glass is sealed at the edges. When that seal fails, humid air gets in and condenses between the panes. It can't be wiped off. The fix is a new sealed unit installed in your existing frame.

Is condensation on the inside of the glass the same problem?

No. Condensation on the room side in winter is usually caused by high indoor humidity, not a broken window. If you can wipe it off, the seal is fine. We'll tell you honestly if a repair won't help.

Do you need to replace the frame?

Usually not. If the frame is in good condition we replace only the glass or the broken part.

How long does a new sealed unit take?

Glass is made to measure, so there are a few days between measuring and installing. The installation itself is usually quick.

Do you work on all brands?

Yes. We repair windows and patio doors from most manufacturers, in homes and commercial buildings.
